About Me

Hi, I'm Flo Bischoff! I was born in Halle (Saale), Germany, in 1978.

After completing my Abitur in 1999, I studied Precision Engineering and Microtechnology at Munich University of Applied Sciences. Since 2008, I have worked in the field of intellectual property, and I have been a European Patent Attorney since 2015.

But there is much more to life than work. Outside my professional world, I enjoy playing the guitar, riding motorcycles, cooking, travelling, poetry and photography. Each of these interests gives me a different way to be creative, explore new perspectives and enjoy life.

This website is my personal space to share those passions, experiences and moments with you. Welcome, and enjoy exploring!
